IMDb Datasets

Source: https://datasets.imdbws.com/

These are the IMDb data files available for download. They are provided for non-commercial use only.

Available datasets:

- name.basics.tsv.gz
  Contains information about actors, directors, writers and other people in IMDb

- title.akas.tsv.gz
  Alternative titles for movies and TV shows in different languages/regions

- title.basics.tsv.gz
  Core information about titles (movies, TV shows, episodes) with metadata like genres, runtime, year

- title.crew.tsv.gz
  Director and writer information for each title

- title.episode.tsv.gz
  TV episode information and season/episode numbering

- title.principals.tsv.gz
  Principal cast and crew members for each title (actors, directors, writers)

- title.ratings.tsv.gz
  User ratings and vote counts for titles

Note: As of March 18, 2024 the datasets are backed by a new data source. If you encounter any issues, contact imdb-data-interest@imdb.com
